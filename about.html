Communication Designer — Melbourne

Design is how I make ideas legible, credible, and compelling.

I'm Nick Toh — a communication designer with over 10 years in the creative industry. I've spent the last five of those working closely with startups and small businesses, helping them find and hold a distinctive visual voice.

My work sits across branding, digital, and UI/UX — not because I can't commit, but because good communication design rarely respects those boundaries. A brand system only works if it carries through to the screen. A product only feels right if the visual language is doing its job.

I work best when there's a real problem to solve and a client who cares about getting it right.

What I do

Brand Identity

Logo design, visual identity systems, brand guidelines, and the language that holds it all together.

Digital Design

Websites, landing pages, and digital touchpoints designed for clarity, speed, and conversion.

UI / UX Design

User research, wireframes, prototypes, and interface design that prioritises real-world usability.

Print & Packaging

Publications, packaging, and printed collateral designed to the same standard as everything else.

Art Direction

Visual storytelling, creative direction, and campaign concepts for brands with something to say.

Design Strategy

Positioning, naming, and the bigger thinking that sits behind strong creative work.
