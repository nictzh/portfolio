01 — Brief

The problem

Describe the brief and the core problem the client came with. What did they need? What was the context — market position, audience, constraints? What did success look like from their side?

Keep it honest. If the brief changed, say so here or in the process section.

02 — Process

Research & exploration

Walk through how you approached the problem. Research, competitive analysis, audience insight, early concepts. Show the thinking, not just the outcome.

What patterns did you notice? What assumptions did you test or challenge?

03 — Decisions

Key decisions and why

This is the most important section. Document the choices that shaped the outcome — and the reasoning behind them. What did you choose not to do? What trade-offs did you make?

Avoid describing what the work looks like. Explain why it is the way it is.

04 — Outcome

The result

Present the final work with context. What was delivered? How was it received? Any measurable impact — conversion, retention, brand recognition, press, awards?

If you can't share numbers, describe the qualitative outcome: what the client said, how the brand is being used, what changed.

05 — Learnings

What I learned

What would you do differently? What did this project teach you that carried into future work? Next steps if the project continues.

This section builds trust — it shows you reflect on your work, not just present it.
